HEALTH Backup Privacy Policy

HEALTH Backup is a private, single-owner backup utility used only by its owner.

Google user data

Google Drive metadata and file contents are accessed only to copy, verify, back up, and recover files between Google Drive accounts controlled by the owner.

Use and sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, profiling, or unrelated analytics.

Storage and retention

Backup data is stored only in destinations selected and controlled by the owner. OAuth credentials are kept only in private configuration or secret storage used by the backup workflow.

Revocation

The owner can revoke Google account access at any time in Google Account security settings.
